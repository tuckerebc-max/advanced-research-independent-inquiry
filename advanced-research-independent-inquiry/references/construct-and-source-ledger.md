# Construct and source ledger

**Project snapshot:** 2026-08-11

## Construct boundary

Advanced inquiry extends QUEST with independent question formulation, source synthesis, method fit, ethical planning, evidence analysis, limitations, transparent communication, defense, and revision. It is a composite of inquiry/task frameworks, not a universal PhD or publication standard.

## Release conditions

Close domain-specific methods/ethics, age-appropriate human-subject and privacy treatment, open-science transfer, and oral-defense calibration. Use fictional or low-risk data when approvals are unavailable.
